Black soil is rich in clay content and retains moisture.
Black soil is suitable for cotton, wheat, soybean and sugarcane.

Red soil is suitable for groundnut, millet and pulses.

Rice requires high water availability and is suitable during rainy seasons.

Wheat grows well in cool climates with moderate rainfall.

Cotton requires warm climate and black soil.

Irrigation should be based on soil moisture and crop requirements.

Farmers should avoid overwatering because it can damage plant roots.

During heavy rainfall, farmers should provide proper drainage.

Temperature, rainfall and humidity affect crop growth.

Organic farming improves soil health and maintains nutrients.
